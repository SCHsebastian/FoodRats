Delete your account & data

Developer: Sebastián Cardona · App: FoodRats (es.schsebastian.foodrats)

Delete from inside the app Recommended

  1. Open FoodRats and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and the data below are permanently removed, you are signed out, and analytics data tied to your device is reset.

Can't access the app?

Email schsebastiancardonahenao@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google sign-in, with the subject "FoodRats account deletion". We process the request within 30 days.
