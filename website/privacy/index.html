The short version TL;DR

  • FoodRats is a closed-group meal-sharing app. Your meals are only visible to the crews you choose — never the public.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Analytics and diagnostics are off by default and only turn on if you opt in.
  • Meal photo recognition runs on your device — your plate isn't uploaded just to be classified.
  • You can delete your account and data any time, from inside the app or by email.

Who we are

FoodRats ("the app", "we") is a closed-group meal-sharing app for crews of 3–8 people, made by Sebastián Cardona ("the developer"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, how it's used, and the choices you have. It applies to the FoodRats Android and iOS apps and this website.

For any privacy question, contact schsebastiancardonahenao@gmail.com.

On-device meal recognition

When you snap a plate, FoodRats suggests likely ingredients using a food-recognition model that runs entirely on your device. Your photo is not uploaded to a server for classification. A photo only leaves your device when you choose — when you publish a meal to one or more crews, or when you share a meal card to another app.

How your data is shared

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Your meals, comments, and ratings are visible only to members of the crews you post them to.

To run the app we rely on Google Firebase as a data processor (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, and Analytics) and on Google Sign-In for authentication. These providers process data on our instructions to deliver the service, not for their own independent purposes. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Analytics & consent

Product analytics and crash diagnostics are disabled by default. They are activated only if you explicitly opt in inside the app. The events we record are designed to contain no personally identifying information and are never used to track you across other apps or websites. You can change your choice at any time, and revoking consent stops further collection.

Keeping your data & deleting it

We keep your data while your account is active so the app can work. You can remove it whenever you want:

Delete from inside the app Recommended

  1. Open FoodRats and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Can't access the app?

Email schsebastiancardonahenao@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google sign-in. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

Deletion removes your account profile (including your avatar), the meals and plate photos you posted (across every crew), your comments and ratings, your registered devices, and your crew memberships, and it resets analytics identifiers tied to your device. For the full breakdown, see the account & data deletion page. Some data may persist briefly in encrypted backups and is purged on the normal backup rotation; aggregate, non-identifying data that cannot be tied back to you may be retained.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to crew content is restricted to crew members through server-side security rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we design the app to collect the minimum needed and to keep your meals private to your crews.

Children

FoodRats is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR or similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for optional processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or by emailing us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, surface a notice in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
