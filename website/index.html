What you get

FoodRats is built for one thing: keeping a small group of friends close around food.

Post your plate

Snap the meal, add a line about it, share it to one crew or several. That's the whole ritual.

A feed that's only your people

No strangers, no algorithm, no ads. Just today's meals from the crew you chose.

Streaks & leaderboards

Keep your posting streak hot and see who's topping the crew's week — friendly, not competitive.

Rate without the pressure

Give a plate a quiet score. Blind voting keeps it honest and kind.

Cuisine passport

Every dish stamps your passport. Watch your crew's map of flavors fill in over time.

Yours, privately

On-device meal recognition, no public profile, and one-tap account & data deletion. Always.

How it works

Three steps, then it's just a habit.

Start or join a crew

Make a crew and share the invite link, or tap a friend's link to join theirs. 3–8 people, that's it.

Post what you eat

A photo and a few words. Choose which crews see it. Done in seconds, even offline.

Keep the table warm

React, score, and chase streaks. A weekly recap card sums up the crew's best plates.

Food, not numbers.

FoodRats isn't a diet tracker. There are no calories, no macros, no "good" and "bad" food, and no audience full of strangers. It's just the meals you're proud of, shared with a handful of people who actually care.
