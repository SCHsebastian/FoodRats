The Structural
Redesign

Every FoodRats screen rebuilt on one idea: a continuous edge-to-edge media floor, with information floating above it on a distinct Z-axis. No bounding boxes, no solid dividers — zones are read through depth, alignment, and an extreme jump in type scale. Same Iron & Ember brand, same Fr* vocabulary, same voice — a radically different structure.

FoodRats — closed-group meal-sharing for crews of 3–8. A structural concept exploration, not shipped product. Brand DNA (Iron & Ember, Plus Jakarta Sans, ubiquitous language Meal · Plate · Score · Crew · Member) preserved; the layout system is new — one deliberate departure: frosted translucent strata replace matte concrete cards, which is the point of the brief.
